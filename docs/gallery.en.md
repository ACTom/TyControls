# Gallery

Every example, one picture per tab page, in light and in dark. English UI, 100% scaling.

> 中文版见 [gallery.md](gallery.md)。

The pictures are generated; rerun `powershell -File scripts/make-gallery.ps1` after changing an example.

## Skins

The same demo window in every built-in skin, plus green, the photo theme in `themes/`.
